Raseed — operations surfaces

One board, two surfaces: the office desktop app and the booker PWA. Light only. Every colour below resolves to a token in :root; every status is one of five matched StatusPill pairs. The loop: the booker calls on shops and captures the order, the office confirms and invoices, then the booker delivers and collects the cash. Payments are cash advance or partial only — no installments, no credit terms.
